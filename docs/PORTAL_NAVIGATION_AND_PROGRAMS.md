# Portal navigation and programs

Portal routes show a neutral loading or unavailable screen until customer branding is verified, instead of falling back to the platform navigation. Signed-out visitors on member-only portal screens receive a sign-in link that retains the portal address. Branding failures do not display stale customer branding or imply successful access checks.

Customer context now follows course-to-community links, community-to-personal-message links, inbox/thread navigation, profile sign-in/sign-out and profile My Learning links. Embedded SCORM sign-in keeps available portal context. Course community branding is resolved against its actual course and current portal membership. Personal messages remain account-wide, and shared course communities may include eligible learners from other organizations; the portal screens explicitly explain this. No company-private chat service or message migration is implied. Platform administration and developer-key links are omitted from the portal profile, while their underlying permissions remain unchanged.

Single-course checkout accepts an optional validated portal slug. Before free enrolment or a Stripe request, the server verifies membership and that the course belongs in the selected published portal. Success and cancellation URLs are constructed from the configured HTTPS application origin and retain the slug. The amount, currency, course and learner are still determined on the server. The return URL's `enrolled` marker grants no access; existing payment verification remains authoritative. Tests use mocked Stripe responses and do not create sessions, orders or payments. The hosted Stripe payment screen retains its existing merchant branding; customer-specific Stripe accounts/branding are not introduced.

Migration 0079 adds a portal Programs view with pagination and customer-scoped progress/award entry points. A current portal membership and an active assignment through that organization's cohort are required, even for learners who also belong to other organizations. Other-company-owned programs or courses are rejected. Program completion delegates to the existing server verification of current course certificates and immutable evidence; repeated claims reuse the existing record. Program records remain historical completion records, rather than newly introduced branded PDF credentials. Program course links retain portal context.

Verification: 314 unit tests passed, 17 environment-dependent tests skipped. Type checking and the production build passed. Migration 0079 was tested with rollback-only synthetic fixtures, installed, then tested again. Checks cover tenant separation for a dual-organization learner, incomplete award denial, successful verified/idempotent completion, outsiders, former members, pagination and preservation of the other organization's assignment after removal. Existing 0067 company-program management, 0077 portal and 0078 certificate-branding SQL checks passed after installation. No customer messages were sent and no email or AI providers were called. Browser journeys, deployed application version, printing and actual payment-provider round trips remain unverified.

Remaining white-label work includes browser acceptance checks, custom domains and branded email activation (sender details/API key were deferred). Company-isolated discussion/messaging, custom program-award templates and per-customer payment-provider branding would be additional capabilities, not effects of carrying portal navigation context.
